Statistical properties

Quick facts

Key properties of the standard normal distribution.

μ
Mean
μ = 0 — the distribution is centred at zero.
σ
Standard deviation
σ = 1 — one unit equals one standard deviation.
⟷
Symmetry
P(Z ≤ −z) = P(Z ≥ z). The curve is perfectly symmetric about zero.
68
Empirical rule
68.27% within ±1σ · 95.45% within ±2σ · 99.73% within ±3σ
∫
Total area
The total area under the curve equals 1 (100%).
